# Async prereading: cold and warm startup

Batch `20260925-212448-6a99b2` completed 160 measured launches and 20 warmups. Each build/storage/startup-condition combination has 20 measured samples. All samples passed validation; recomputed P50/P90 values match the recorded results.

The comparison uses Windows x64 Release builds: the unmodified Electron baseline at [`56f681cfe0`](https://github.com/electron/electron/commit/56f681cfe08e1c6bf02cfad2807ff1c47ae13d4f) and the split build with post-load background prereading corresponding to [`5406e2db70`](https://github.com/zuohuiyang/electron/commit/5406e2db70774292bc1f017949029b685fa21a29). Build records and deployed binary hashes are in [provenance](../provenance/async-20260925/build-record.json).

Timings start immediately before the parent calls `spawn()`, on the validated QPC clock axis. Both builds use this origin, which includes synchronous process-creation work. The endpoints are APP READY and the first visible video presentation callback from the same local-video app. They do not measure physical screen scanout.

| Storage | Startup | Endpoint | Baseline P50 | Async P50 | Change | Baseline P90 | Async P90 | Change |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SSD | Cold | APP READY | 469 | 225 | −244 ms (−52.1%) | 486 | 238 | −248 ms (−51.1%) |
| SSD | Cold | Video callback | 830 | 582 | −249 ms (−29.9%) | 853 | 608 | −245 ms (−28.8%) |
| HDD | Cold | APP READY | 3430 | 2099 | −1330 ms (−38.8%) | 3525 | 2247 | −1278 ms (−36.3%) |
| HDD | Cold | Video callback | 8098 | 7633 | −465 ms (−5.7%) | 8412 | 8055 | −357 ms (−4.2%) |
| SSD | Warm | APP READY | 63 | 67 | +4 ms (+5.6%) | 68 | 94 | +26 ms (+38.0%) |
| SSD | Warm | Video callback | 220 | 223 | +3 ms (+1.5%) | 233 | 240 | +6 ms (+2.7%) |
| HDD | Warm | APP READY | 64 | 66 | +2 ms (+3.3%) | 78 | 94 | +17 ms (+21.6%) |
| HDD | Warm | Video callback | 221 | 217 | −4 ms (−1.6%) | 230 | 254 | +23 ms (+10.0%) |

All times are in ms. Differences and percentages are calculated before rounding, so displayed integer values may differ by 1 ms from their displayed difference. Negative changes are faster.

Cold-start P50 and P90 improved for both endpoints on both drives. Warm-start APP READY P50 still increased by 2–4 ms. All four warm-start P90 metrics also increased, so this batch does not establish regression-free warm startup. With 20 samples per group, P90 depends on a small number of slower launches; their cause has not been isolated in this batch.

The machine used a Ryzen 9 7950X, 32 GiB RAM, Windows 11 Pro build 26200, a Samsung 990 PRO SSD and a Seagate ST4000VX000 HDD. The test ran under the non-elevated `autotest` account. Each build was copied to a fresh directory on each drive. Cold samples ran first, one per reboot, followed by warmups and warm samples in the last boot session. Windows caching and prefetch settings were retained. Input hashes were checked after measurement, not before cold launches. See the [environment](../data/async-20260925/environment.json) and [configuration](../data/async-20260925/config.json).

The older [synchronous-preread batch](RESULTS.zh-CN.md) used a different Electron baseline and the process-creation timestamp. Its reported warm-start APP READY overhead was about 20 ms. The current 2–4 ms result uses the new parent origin and a new batch; subtracting the two is not a controlled measurement of the code change alone. The new harness also disables background throttling for both builds and preserves a narrowly allowlisted AMD driver probe message in raw stderr.

Raw samples, idle checks, logs, the recorded report, and the sampling-time harness/controller snapshots are in [async-20260925](../data/async-20260925). `SHA256.json` preserves their original bytes. The Electron binaries, native clock helper executable and profile copies are omitted; their hashes remain in `inputs.json`. The video fixture is already included at `code/harness/app/local-video.mp4`. The [build record](../provenance/async-20260925/build-record.json) describes how the tested EXE and uninstrumented DLL were assembled.

From the repository root, verify the archived file hashes and recompute all eight comparisons without launching Electron:

```powershell
node code/scripts/analyze-run.cjs
```

To collect a new batch, use [code/run.ps1](../../code/run.ps1) with your Release build paths and SSD/HDD directories. It defaults to cold-first, 20 samples per condition and 5 warmups per path. See the [running guide](../../doc/RUNNING.zh-CN.md).
